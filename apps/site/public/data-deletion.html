PRIVACY

Data Deletion

You can ask us to delete your NegosyoOnline account and the personal information we hold about you. This applies to every account, including accounts you created with Facebook or Google.

How to ask

  1. Email support@negosyoonline.com with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Send it from the email address on your NegosyoOnline account, so we can confirm the account is yours. If you signed up with Facebook or Google, use the email address that account uses.
  3. We will reply within 1 business day to confirm the request, complete the deletion within 30 calendar days, and tell you when it is done.

What we delete

We delete or anonymise your name, email address, mobile number, and the sign-in link to Facebook or Google. We do not store your Facebook or Google password or access token. All of your websites and their content are removed with your account. The personal information of a Subscriber’s customers is anonymised together with the website.

If you are a Subscriber, you can also ask us to erase the personal information of one of your customers. The money facts of that customer’s orders, such as amounts, dates, and payment references, stay without the person’s details.

Before your website is deleted, you can ask us for an export of your store data. Email support@negosyoonline.com from your account email.

What deleting your account ends

If a website is inside a paid period, deleting your account cancels its subscription: the website stays on until the end of that period, no refund is given for the rest of it, and no further renewal is charged. Its data is deleted after that. Any unpaid eligible usage from a website’s last paid billing cycle is still owed, as described in our Terms of Service. Unused Subscription Credits from the Referral Program are forfeited. An included .com domain stays with its registrant and is not transferred to you.

What we may keep

We may keep billing, payment, and tax records, security logs, backups, fraud-prevention records, and records needed to resolve disputes or follow the law, for as long as those obligations require. These records are not used for anything else. Billing and tax records, including the personal information on them, are kept for at least 5 years as described in our Privacy Policy and are not changed or blanked on request before then. After that period ends, the personal information in them is removed.

If a final bill is still unpaid after the deletion date, we keep your account’s name, sign-in email, and business name so that you can still sign in and pay it, and we delete or anonymise those details once it is settled. The Billing Statement itself is kept as described above.

Facebook and Google

Deleting your NegosyoOnline account does not delete your Facebook or Google account. To stop NegosyoOnline from receiving your profile details from them in the future, you can also remove NegosyoOnline from your Facebook “Apps and Websites” settings or from your Google Account’s “Sign in with Google” settings.

For more about how we handle personal information, read our Privacy Policy.
