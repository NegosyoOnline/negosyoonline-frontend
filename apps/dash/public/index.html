Your store profile and web address

Open Settings to see Business Settings for your site. The Site readiness list at the top shows what is done and what is still needed. It does not switch anything on or off.

  • Business identity. Your business name, site name and business address. Each has an Edit button. The site name is shown at the top of your site and in this dashboard.
  • Customer contact. Your store email, store mobile and social links, so customers can reach you. They are not your sign-in email or mobile.
  • Public and Private. Public means customers can see it on your site. Private means only you and NegosyoOnline see it.
  • Saving. Each Edit window saves its own section when you press Save.

Your web address

You chose your web address when you created your site: 3 to 30 lowercase letters, digits or single hyphens. It is reserved for your site and is never given to another site. It cannot be changed on the screen. To change it, email support@negosyoonline.com.

Santé setup

Everything for your Santé site is on Business Settings.

  • Santé Username. In Business identity. New members who register through your site are registered under it. Use letters, digits and hyphens, up to 32 characters. Member registration on your site cannot start until you add it.
  • Legal page details. Your registered business name, DTI business name number, BIR Official Control Number, TIN and privacy contact email. They are printed on your site’s legal pages, except your TIN. These are not your NegosyoOnline invoice details; those are in Billing.
  • BIR registration badge. Upload a JPEG, PNG or WebP image, up to 2 MB. It is shown in the registration block at the bottom of your site. You can replace or remove it. Only the account owner can change it.
  • Delivery estimates and Shipping Policy. The estimates are suggested values. Your Shipping Policy stays offline until you review them: change anything that does not match how you ship, tick that you reviewed them, and save.
  • Membership Terms. Buyers review and agree to the Santé Membership Terms when they complete their account details after paying.

Payment accounts and your QR code

Open Settings, then Payments. Buyers send the amount to one of your accounts by bank or e-wallet transfer and upload their proof. You verify each payment.

  1. Press Add account.
  2. Choose Bank or E-wallet. For a bank, also choose Savings or Checking.
  3. Enter the bank or e-wallet name, the account name and the account number.
  4. Optional: add a QR code image (JPEG, PNG or WebP) that buyers can scan. Drag the square over your QR code so only the code is kept, then press Use this area.
  5. Press Add account to save.
  • Buyers who choose transfer see your accounts in full at checkout. This page shows only the last 4 digits of each number.
  • Use Change to edit an account or replace its QR code. Remove asks you to confirm.
  • The Bank or e-wallet transfer switch can be turned on once you have added an account. If no payment method is showing, buyers cannot check out.

Reviewing Membership Package orders and their proof of payment

Open Orders, then the Package Order tab. Each order is for one Membership Pack.

  • Under review orders wait in the Payment review queue, newest proof first. Needs attention orders were rejected. Verified orders were approved. Press a count to filter the list, and press it again to clear the filter.
  • Press Details on an order to see the payment proof (an image or a PDF; you can open or download it), the buyer’s name and masked mobile, the account the buyer paid to, and the payment details the buyer entered: order total, amount sent, reference number, bank, sender name and date sent. The History shows each proof and each decision.

Approve

Check that the amount sent matches the order total, the reference number matches, and the money is in the account the buyer paid to. Then press Approve. The order moves to Verified.

Reject

  1. Press Reject and choose a reason: the amount does not match, the payment was not found in the receiving account, the proof is unreadable or incomplete, the reference number is missing or does not match, it was sent to the wrong account, or Other. Other needs a written explanation of up to 500 characters.
  2. Press Continue, check the summary, then press Confirm Reject.

What the buyer sees. The buyer is told their payment for that order needs attention. They see your reason when they sign in and can send a new proof. A rejected order can still be approved later.

Paying your NegosyoOnline plan by bank transfer with proof

Open Billing from the Settings tabs. You pay for your plan by bank transfer and send us a proof of payment. We review the proof and confirm your payment. A payment counts only when we confirm it.

Pay for your plan

  1. On Billing, press Pay by bank transfer. If you have paid before, the button says Pay for the next period.
  2. Choose Monthly or Annual and press Choose. Annual is paid once for 12 months.
  3. The next page shows the account to pay to: the bank or e-wallet, the account name and the account number. Make the transfer to that account.
  4. Fill in Your payment details: the sender bank, the reference number of your transfer, the sender’s first and last name, the sender account number, the amount and the time of the transfer. Upload your payment receipt as a JPEG, PNG or PDF file, up to 5 MB.
  5. Press Send proof.

After you send the proof

  • Proof under review: we have your proof and are checking it. Press View proof to see what you sent.
  • Approved: we confirmed your payment.
  • Proof rejected: Billing shows the reason. Your money has not been counted. Press Send a new proof, or contact us if you have already paid the right amount.

Payment history on Billing lists every proof you sent as Under review, Approved, Rejected or Replaced.

Replace a proof

While a proof is under review, press Replace proof and send the details and the receipt again. The new proof replaces the one under review, and the old one shows as Replaced in Payment history.

When your free trial ends

  • If a proof you sent is still under review when your free trial ends, your storefront and dashboard stay on for 7 calendar days after the trial ends. If we confirm your payment within those 7 days, your paid period starts from the day your trial ended. If it is not confirmed within those 7 days, your site is paused until it is.
  • If no proof is under review when your free trial ends, your service ends when the trial ends.

If a payment is late

  • Billing shows the date your next payment is due.
  • If your payment is not confirmed by the due date, your storefront and dashboard stay fully on for 7 calendar days after the due date, and you can still pay. If we confirm your payment within those 7 days, your next period still counts from the original due date, with no extra free days.
  • If your payment is not confirmed within 7 days after the due date, your site is paused until it is. A proof still under review does not extend the 7 days. While your site is paused, your storefront and dashboard are off, and Billing stays available so you can pay.
  • To bring your site back, pay from Billing as above. When we confirm your payment, your site is back and a new period starts at the moment of that payment.

The dates for downloading your data and for coming back are in Support, your data and leaving.

Support, your data and leaving

Contact support

Email support@negosyoonline.com or call or text +63 917 309 8619. Include your account email, your business name, what happened and when, and any Billing Statement or payment reference. Please do not put a sign-in code in an email.

  • We confirm we received your message within 1 business day and give you a reference number.
  • Within 7 business days of your complaint, we resolve it or explain in writing what we found, what we will do, and when.
  • If you are not satisfied, reply with your reference number and ask for escalation. The founder gives a final written response within 7 business days of your request.
  • Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding Philippine public holidays.

The full process is in the Terms of Service.

Your data

To get an export of your store data, email support from your account email. In Billing, Export data on a cancelled site opens that email. To delete your account and personal information, see Data Deletion: we reply within 1 business day and complete it within 30 calendar days. Billing and tax records, including your Billing Statements, are kept for at least 5 years.

When a subscription ends

Each site has its own subscription. A site’s service ends when you cancel, when a trial ends with no payment method, or when a payment is still unpaid 7 days after its due date and the subscription is suspended. When it ends:

  • Your site is no longer shown to customers, and the dashboard is limited to Billing.
  • You have 30 calendar days from the end of the service to download your store data. Your records are kept meanwhile.
  • You can reactivate before 90 calendar days from the end of the service (30 days for a site that never had a successful payment) by choosing a plan, adding a payment method and paying. After that, the site’s store data is deleted or anonymised and the site cannot be reactivated.

Billing shows the exact dates for your site. The details are in the Terms of Service and the Cancellation and Refund Policy.
