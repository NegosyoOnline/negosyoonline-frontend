Scope

This Policy applies to subscriptions purchased directly from NegosyoOnline IT Solutions for the NegosyoOnline platform.

It does not apply to refunds between a Subscriber and the Subscriber’s own customers. Each Subscriber is responsible for its store’s product, delivery, cancellation, return, and customer-refund policies.

Words such as “Subscriber” have the meanings given in the Terms of Service.

Free Trial

Each website receives a 14-day free trial. The trial for your first website starts when your account is created, and the trial for each website you add later starts when you add it. Each trial ends exactly 14 calendar days after it starts. No card or other payment method is required to start the trial, and you are not charged during the trial.

You may cancel at any time during the trial. No charge is due, and cancellation ends the service immediately.

If no payment method is active for a website when its trial ends, that website’s subscription ends as “Cancelled – no payment method.” Nothing is charged and no billing statement is issued. The data and reactivation rules below then apply.

Monthly and Annual Subscriptions and Renewal

Billing is per website. Each website is one online shop with its own subscription, trial, plan, payment method, billing cycle or annual term, and renewal date, and each website is cancelled on its own. Cancelling one website does not cancel or change the Subscriber’s other websites. NegosyoOnline IT Solutions is not registered for VAT, so no VAT is added to these prices.

Each website is on one of two plans:

  • Monthly Plan: PHP 2,499 per month, paid at the start of each monthly billing cycle.
  • Annual Plan: PHP 24,990 for a 12-month annual term, paid in advance in one payment. This equals 10 monthly payments, so the Subscriber gets 2 months free and saves PHP 4,998, or 16.7%, compared with 12 monthly payments.

The first payment for the chosen plan is due when the trial ends, through the payment options shown in Billing, and the first billing cycle (or, on the Annual Plan, the first annual term) starts when that payment counts, as described in the Terms of Service. On the Monthly Plan, each later billing cycle starts at 3:00 PM Asia/Manila time on the renewal date, the same calendar date each month as the start of the first billing cycle or the latest reactivation (or that month’s last day when the date does not exist), when the renewal payment is collected. On the Annual Plan, the term renews at 3:00 PM Asia/Manila time on the same calendar date 12 months after it started, when the next PHP 24,990 payment is collected.

Unless cancelled, the subscription renews automatically on the same plan. A change between the Monthly Plan and the Annual Plan takes effect only at the next renewal, and never ends, shortens, or refunds a period already paid for.

An annual term is made up of 12 monthly allowance periods, each with its own allowances, which reset each month and do not carry over.

Every Billing Statement, including an annual statement, is an Internal Billing Statement — Not an Official BIR Receipt or BIR Electronic Invoice.

Cancellation, Data Download, and Reactivation

A Subscriber may cancel a website at any time, in Billing or by contacting support@negosyoonline.com. The effect depends on when the Subscriber cancels:

  • During the free trial: no charge is due. Cancellation ends the service immediately.
  • During first-payment recovery, where no payment has ever succeeded: cancellation ends the service immediately. No subscription fee, usage charge, or Billing Statement is due.
  • During renewal recovery after a previously paid billing cycle: cancellation ends the 7-day recovery period and the service immediately. The pending renewal fee (PHP 2,499 on the Monthly Plan or PHP 24,990 on the Annual Plan) is not due.
  • During an active paid billing cycle or annual term: cancellation stops the next renewal. It does not end access immediately. The Subscriber retains access until the end of the already-paid billing cycle or annual term, with no prorated refund for unused time. A cancellation cannot be withdrawn once submitted; to keep the website after the paid cycle or term ends, the Subscriber reactivates it as described below.
  • While the subscription is suspended: cancellation ends the service on the date of cancellation. The unpaid cycle is not charged.

The service ends at the end of the last paid billing cycle or annual term after a cancellation during a paid cycle or term, at the end of the trial when no payment method is active, or on the date the Subscriber cancels during the trial or during payment recovery, or on the day the subscription is suspended for non-payment, if it is not reactivated. If the Subscriber cancels while suspended, the day of the suspension stays the end of the service for its data. From that date the Subscriber has 30 calendar days to download its store data. To get the data, email support@negosyoonline.com from the account email and we will send an export of the store data.

Before 90 calendar days from the end of the service (30 calendar days for a website whose free trial ended without any payment), the Subscriber can reactivate by choosing the Monthly Plan or the Annual Plan and completing a successful payment. A new billing cycle or annual term starts at the moment of that payment.

At 90 calendar days (30 calendar days for a website that never had a successful payment) from the end of the service, NegosyoOnline deletes or anonymises that website’s store data, and the account data if the Subscriber has no other website, subject to legal, billing, tax, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, and backup-retention requirements. After the reactivation period ends, the website cannot be reactivated. The Subscriber can add a new website to its account, or create a new account if it has no other website.

No Prorated Refunds

Subscription fees are paid in advance for the applicable monthly billing cycle or 12-month annual term.

NegosyoOnline does not provide prorated refunds or credits for unused monthly or annual time, partial use of a billing cycle or annual term, unused features, or a change of mind after payment has been made. This includes cancelling an annual term before it ends. The only exceptions are the cases listed under When a Refund May Be Approved, subject to applicable law.

When a Refund May Be Approved

A refund may be approved when NegosyoOnline confirms one of the following:

  • A duplicate charge.
  • A billing error made by NegosyoOnline.
  • NegosyoOnline’s own unresolved platform failure prevented delivery of the paid service.

Refunds are assessed case by case. Nothing in this Policy limits any consumer right or remedy that cannot legally be excluded.

When a Refund Will Not Normally Be Approved

Refunds will not normally be approved for:

  • A change of mind or unused subscription time.
  • A Subscriber’s failure to cancel before renewal.
  • Subscriber configuration errors, account-access issues caused by the Subscriber, or loss of credentials.
  • Unpaid, expired, incorrectly configured, or third-party-managed domains.
  • Problems caused by the Subscriber’s products, fulfilment, courier, payment provider, internet connection, or third-party service.
  • Suspension or termination caused by a breach of the Terms of Service, fraud, unlawful activity, security abuse, or prohibited use.
  • Custom development, bespoke integrations, or separately agreed professional services, unless a written agreement states otherwise.

Refund Timing

If a refund is approved, NegosyoOnline will process it within 10 business days. The time for funds to appear may vary depending on the original payment method or payment provider.

Approved refunds will normally be returned to the original payment method, unless this is not possible or the parties agree otherwise.

Failed Payments and Suspension

If a payment fails, whether it is the first payment at the end of the trial or a renewal, the subscription shows “Payment Requires Attention” for 7 calendar days from the scheduled payment date. During this time the storefront and dashboard stay on, and the Subscriber can pay through the payment options shown in Billing, or cancel (see Cancellation above).

If payment succeeds within the 7 days, the billing cycle counts from its original start date, with no extra free days.

If payment has not succeeded by the end of the 7 days, the subscription is suspended. The storefront and dashboard are turned off, but Billing stays available. The unpaid cycle is not charged, and no usage charges apply to it. Records are kept while the website is suspended, so the Subscriber can pay and reactivate. If the Subscriber does not reactivate before 90 days from the suspension (30 days if the website never had a successful payment), the website’s store data is anonymised and the website can no longer be reactivated.

To reactivate a suspended subscription, complete a successful payment through the payment options shown in Billing. That payment covers the subscription fee for the new monthly billing cycle or annual term. A new billing cycle or annual term starts at the moment of that payment. A suspended Subscriber may also cancel. The 30-day download and 90-day deletion periods above still count from the date of the suspension (30 days for a website that never had a successful payment); cancelling does not extend them.
