1. Program Overview

The NegosyoOnline Referral Program lets eligible subscribers receive Subscription Credits when their direct referrals make qualifying NegosyoOnline subscription payments.

This is a Subscription Credit program only. It is not an income opportunity, investment program, commission plan, team-building program, or cash-reward program.

These Referral Program Terms form part of our Terms of Service. Words such as “Subscriber,” “billing cycle,” and “Payment Requires Attention” have the meanings given in the Terms of Service.

2. Subscription Credit Amount

For each qualifying direct referral payment, an eligible referrer receives a Subscription Credit equal to 20% of the amount actually paid for base subscriptions, rounded to the nearest peso.

The Subscription Credit is based only on the base-subscription amount paid after any Subscription Credits have been applied. It does not include usage charges, payment-gateway fees, third-party fees, domain charges, taxes, custom work, or other pass-through charges.

Each website is billed separately and has its own base subscription: PHP 2,499 a month on the Monthly Plan, or PHP 24,990 for a 12-month annual term on the Annual Plan. When a referred subscriber has more than one website, each website’s qualifying base-subscription payment creates its own Subscription Credit.

An annual payment is one qualifying payment. A full verified PHP 24,990 Annual Plan payment creates one Subscription Credit of PHP 4,998 for the eligible direct referrer. It is one credit for the annual term, not 12 monthly credits, and the monthly allowance periods of that term create no further credits.

Example: Ana refers Ben. Ben chooses the Annual Plan and pays PHP 24,990, with no Subscription Credits applied. Once NegosyoOnline has received and processed Ben’s verified successful payment, and Ana meets the conditions in section 4, Ana receives one PHP 4,998 Subscription Credit (20% of PHP 24,990). If Ben had used PHP 2,000 of his own Subscription Credits and paid PHP 22,990, Ana would receive PHP 4,598 (20% of PHP 22,990).

All Subscription Credits a referrer earns go into one Subscription Credit balance for the referrer’s account. That balance is shared by all of the referrer’s websites and applies to whichever of their websites renews next, as described in section 5.

3. No Cap and Carry-Forward

There is no limit to the number of people a subscriber may directly refer and no cap on Subscription Credits earned from qualifying direct referral payments.

Subscription Credits carry forward. If the referrer has no eligible charge to apply them to right away, they remain available for future eligible charges: monthly or annual base subscriptions, NegosyoOnline add-ons, and eligible usage-overage charges, as described in sections 5, 6 and 8.

A Subscription Credit is a balance that reduces eligible NegosyoOnline charges. It is not cash, store-sales proceeds, a wallet withdrawal, or a refund to a Subscriber’s customer. Subscription Credits cannot be withdrawn, transferred, exchanged, sold, or applied to another account. They cannot be redeemed for gadgets or other physical goods.

4. When a Subscription Credit Is Created

A Subscription Credit may be created only after NegosyoOnline receives and processes a verified successful payment of a referred subscriber’s qualifying NegosyoOnline base subscription from our approved payment provider. A return to our website from the payment page, a pending payment, a payment reference typed in by anyone, a failed payment, an unverified payment event, or a payment that is refunded, charged back, fraudulent, or otherwise invalid does not create a Subscription Credit.

The referrer must, at the time of that payment:

  • Have at least one website with an active paid subscription that is not suspended or in Payment Requires Attention. On the Monthly Plan, a subscription that has been cancelled does not count.
  • Have no unpaid balance on any of their websites, including an unpaid monthly usage or add-on charge.
  • Not be deactivated or under a fraud-review hold.

A subscriber on the Annual Plan counts as having an active paid subscription for the whole of their paid 12-month annual term, even after they submit a cancellation, because cancelling only stops the next annual renewal. This is subject to the good-standing conditions above: while a monthly usage or add-on charge is unpaid, or the subscription is in Payment Requires Attention, no new Subscription Credit is created until the balance is settled.

A referred subscriber becomes qualifying only after completing any applicable trial and making a verified successful payment of their first base subscription. Future verified successful base-subscription payments, monthly or annual, may continue to create Subscription Credits while the referral and referrer remain eligible.

If the referred subscriber’s base subscriptions are fully covered by their own Subscription Credits, they paid PHP 0 for the base subscriptions. No Subscription Credit is created from that payment.

5. How Subscription Credits Apply

Subscription Credits apply to the base subscription of each of the referrer’s active subscribed websites (PHP 2,499 on the Monthly Plan or PHP 24,990 on the Annual Plan) and to NegosyoOnline add-ons. Because each website is billed on its own dates, the account balance is applied to each website’s eligible charges as they come due.

They do not apply to payment-provider or payment-gateway fees, third-party or pass-through charges, domain charges, taxes, or custom work, and they can never be withdrawn as cash.

Each time a website’s charges come due, the amount due for that website is calculated as:

  • Eligible charges = that website’s base subscription due + its NegosyoOnline add-ons due + its eligible usage-overage charges due
  • Subscription Credits applied = the lower of available Subscription Credits or eligible charges
  • Amount due = eligible charges − Subscription Credits applied + non-creditable charges

Subscription Credits are applied to base subscriptions first, then to NegosyoOnline add-ons, then to eligible usage-overage charges.

If available Subscription Credits fully cover the eligible charges, only the non-creditable charges remain payable.

6. Billing Cycle and Credit Balance

A subscriber’s Subscription Credit balance begins accumulating after their subscription payment succeeds.

Monthly and annual renewal payments are collected at 3:00 PM Asia/Manila time on their collection date, as set out in the Terms of Service. Subscription Credits created before that 3:00 PM Asia/Manila cutoff may be applied to that collection. Subscription Credits created after the cutoff apply to the next eligible charge.

Unused Subscription Credits carry forward from one charge to the next.

7. Direct Referrals Only

A referral link and code become available only once the referrer has an active paid subscription. Before then, the referral link is disabled and no referral can be assigned through it.

A referral must be assigned through the referrer’s unique referral link or code when the new subscriber creates their account.

Referral assignments cannot be changed later.

Only direct referrals qualify. There are no indirect referrals, teams, downlines, matching benefits, or rewards for referrals made by another subscriber.

8. Cancellations, Unpaid Balances, and Suspension

A referrer on the Monthly Plan stops receiving new Subscription Credits immediately when they no longer have a website with an active, uncancelled paid subscription. A referrer on the Annual Plan stays eligible through the full paid annual term, including after submitting a cancellation, subject to the unpaid-balance rule below.

If a referrer has an unpaid balance, including during the 7-day Payment Requires Attention period, they cannot receive new Subscription Credits. A payment by their referral during that time does not create a later Subscription Credit.

If the referrer does not meet the eligibility conditions in section 4 when a referred subscriber pays, no Subscription Credit is created.

Unused Subscription Credits stay on the account while any of its websites is suspended or has ended, and they can be used again once a website is on an active paid subscription. Unused Subscription Credits are forfeited, and cannot be redeemed, when the account is permanently deleted.

9. Reversals and Corrections

NegosyoOnline may reverse, remove, or correct a Subscription Credit where the related referral payment is refunded, reversed, charged back, disputed, fraudulent, or otherwise invalid.

If a reversed Subscription Credit was already applied to a Billing Statement, the equivalent amount is first deducted from the referrer’s available Subscription Credits. Any remaining amount may be added to the next bill of any of the referrer’s websites that has a continuing subscription. It is never billed once none of their websites has a continuing subscription. Subscription Credits are never paid in cash.

10. Name Visibility

For referral tracking and Subscription Credit administration, NegosyoOnline may show a qualifying referred subscriber’s business name to their direct referrer after the referred subscriber makes their first qualifying paid subscription.

NegosyoOnline will not show the referred subscriber’s owner name, email address, mobile number, payment method, Billing Statement details, separate charges, or other personal or account information to the referrer through the Referral Program.

When someone opens a subscribed referrer’s referral link, NegosyoOnline may show the referrer’s first name so the new subscriber knows who referred them.

11. Fair Use and Reviews

Self-referrals and referrals between accounts controlled by the same person, business, payment method, mobile number, or email address are not eligible.

NegosyoOnline may withhold, approve, deny, reverse, or remove Subscription Credits where it reasonably identifies possible duplicate accounts, payment misuse, fraud, abuse, or violations of these Terms.

A subscriber may request a support review by emailing support@negosyoonline.com. NegosyoOnline does not disclose its fraud-prevention methods or review criteria. Complaints follow the complaint process in our Terms of Service.

12. Program Changes

NegosyoOnline may change, suspend, or end the Referral Program where reasonably necessary for operational, security, fraud-prevention, legal, or business reasons. Changes will not convert Subscription Credits into cash or create a payment entitlement.
