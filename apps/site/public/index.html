BUILT FROM FIRSTHAND EXPERIENCE

Built by a business owner who understands the frustration of going online.

NegosyoOnline was founded by Ed Geronilla, a Computer Engineering graduate of Ateneo de Davao University and a Python/Django developer for more than 15 years. From 2011 to 2018 he helped run Datamarked ApS, a Danish e-commerce business, where he led teams and managed suppliers, products, and operations as its Philippine Country Manager.

Even with technical skills, getting a business online still meant too many tools and subscriptions, plus the technical skill to connect them all. NegosyoOnline was created to give Philippine business owners an all-in-one system for selling online, a clearer and more practical path forward.
