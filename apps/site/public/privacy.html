5. Subscriber Store Data

For information about a Subscriber’s customers processed through the Subscriber’s online store, the Subscriber is responsible for deciding why and how that information is collected and used. The Subscriber must provide its customers with an appropriate privacy notice and comply with applicable data-protection obligations.

NegosyoOnline processes that information only as necessary to operate the hosted store, order tools, customer support, security, and approved integrations. We do not sell that information or use it for NegosyoOnline’s own marketing.

NegosyoOnline processes the personal information of a Subscriber’s customers only on the Subscriber’s instructions, keeps it confidential, and follows the Data Processing section of our Terms of Service. If a Subscriber connects its Meta Pixel to its store, events about what a customer does in the store are sent to Meta from the customer’s browser and also from NegosyoOnline’s servers, through Meta’s Conversions API. These events are sent for the Subscriber and under the Subscriber’s own Meta advertising account. Nothing is sent unless the customer accepts the store’s tracking notice. With each event we send a visitor ID that NegosyoOnline creates for that browser and, after a purchase, links to the customer’s account. From the checkout page onward we also send the customer’s email address and mobile number, and with payment and purchase events the customer’s first and last name, city, province and postal code from the order. All of these are sent only in hashed form. The Subscriber must tell its customers about this and obtain their consent where the law requires it.

If a Subscriber connects its Facebook Page to its store’s Inbox, the messages customers send to that Page on Messenger, the store’s replies, and the customer’s Messenger ID for that Page are received from Meta and kept for the Subscriber in its Inbox. They are deleted 180 days after the conversation’s last message, or sooner when the Subscriber’s account is closed or when Meta sends us a data deletion request from the person who connected the Page. The Page’s access token is stored encrypted and is used only to receive and send that Page’s messages.

If a store connects its Facebook Page and turns on comments, we process the comments on that Page’s posts and ads (the commenter’s name, Facebook ID and comment) so the store can read and answer them, including automatic replies the store sets up. We keep them for up to 180 days.

If a store turns on website chat, the messages a customer sends through the chat, the store’s replies, and the name, email address or mobile number the customer chooses to give are kept for the Subscriber in its Inbox. They are deleted 180 days after the conversation’s last message, or sooner when the Subscriber’s account is closed.

If a store turns on text messages (SMS), it can text its own members from its Inbox. The texts come from NegosyoOnline’s mobile number, or from the store’s own paired phone, and start with the store’s name. Texts a store sends from its own paired phone travel through that store’s mobile carrier. A reply goes to the store that last texted you in the past 7 days; otherwise it comes to NegosyoOnline’s own inbox. The texts, the replies and your mobile number are kept for the Subscriber in its Inbox and deleted 180 days after the conversation’s last message, or sooner when the Subscriber’s account is closed. If you reply STOP, that store can no longer text you. Sign-in codes you ask for still arrive.

8. Retention of Information

We retain personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy, including legal, billing, tax, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, and backup requirements.

The service ends at the end of the last paid billing cycle or annual term after a cancellation during a paid cycle or term, at the end of the free trial when no payment method is active, or on the date the Subscriber cancels during the trial or while a payment needs attention, or on the day the subscription is suspended for non-payment, if it is not reactivated. If the Subscriber cancels while suspended, the day of the suspension stays the end of the service for its data. Each website has its own service end. From that date the Subscriber has 30 calendar days to download its store data, which our support team exports and sends on request to the account email. The Subscriber can reactivate the website before 90 calendar days from the end of the service (30 calendar days for a website whose free trial ended without any payment). At 90 calendar days (30 calendar days for a website that never had a successful payment) from the end of the service, we delete or anonymise that website’s store data, and the account data if the Subscriber has no other website. Billing and tax records, including Billing Statements and the personal information on them, are kept for at least 5 years, counted from the day after the filing deadline of the tax return for the year in which the last entry was made. They are kept longer if a tax protest or refund claim is pending and the records are material to it, until the case is resolved. Security logs, backups, and other records required by law are kept for as long as those obligations require.

While a subscription is suspended for non-payment, its records are kept so the Subscriber can reactivate. A suspended Subscriber may cancel at any time, and the 30-day and 90-day periods above count from the date of the suspension; cancelling does not extend them.

If you create an account but never verify your email address and have not paid for a website, we delete or anonymise that account 30 calendar days after you signed up.

We keep other information for these periods:

  • Contact enquiries: 1 year from the enquiry, then deleted.
  • Records of the emails we send you (who we emailed and when, not the text of the email): 2 years, then deleted. Records of emails that deliver a Billing Statement or other billing document are billing records and are kept for at least 5 years, as described above.
  • Text-message (SMS) records, including the text of the message: 1 year, then deleted. If you text STOP we keep your number so we do not text you again; a STOP sent to a store stops that store’s texts.
  • Security records, such as contact-change and recovery checks: 1 year, then the personal details in them are blanked.
  • Sign-up details that include a date of birth, collected through a Subscriber’s store: erased 90 days after the sign-up’s last status change, whatever the state of the subscription.
  • Expired sign-in sessions, one-time codes, and sign-in tokens: deleted 30 days after they expire.
  • Browser identifiers kept for an order’s advertising event: 30 days.
  • Messenger conversations in a store’s Inbox: 180 days after the conversation’s last message, then deleted.
  • Website chat conversations in a store’s Inbox: 180 days after the conversation’s last message, then deleted.
  • Text-message (SMS) conversations in a store’s Inbox: 180 days after the conversation’s last message, then deleted.
